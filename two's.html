Signed Integers & Two's Complement Arithmetic

Computer Science Core Topic

Sign-Magnitude vs. Two's Complement

In binary systems, positive numbers are easy! But how do computers store negative numbers? Sign-Magnitude simply uses the first bit as a negative flag (+/-), while Two's Complement makes arithmetic effortless in hardware by turning subtraction into simple binary addition.

Sign-Magnitude Problem

Has two representations for zero: 00000000 (+0) and 10000000 (-0). Standard binary addition fails with standard logic circuits!

Why Two's Complement Wins

Only one zero (00000000), allows natural hardware addition for both positive & negative integers, and gives an extra negative value range (-128 to +127).

Subtraction Transformation Rule:
A - B ===> A + (-B)

Computers do not have a separate binary subtraction circuit! Instead, to calculate A - B, the system negates B into -B using Two's Complement (Flip bits + 1) and adds it to A.

Carry Out of MSB

When adding 8-bit numbers, any 9th carry bit overflowing past Bit 7 is discarded.

Overflow Flag Status

Overflow occurs only if adding two positive numbers yields a negative result, or adding two negatives yields a positive.
